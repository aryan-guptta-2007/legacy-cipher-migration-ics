
================================================================================================
 LCMF v1.0.0 - Legacy Cipher Migration Framework for ICS: guided demonstration
================================================================================================
Scenario: a distribution utility with eight 11 kV feeders. Each RTU still speaks a historical
cipher. The protocol-translation gateway wraps their telemetry into AES-256-GCM envelopes, and
we measure what can still go wrong during the migration.
